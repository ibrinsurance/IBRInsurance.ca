Before You Book

Answers to what people usually ask first.

Do I need to know what I want already?

No. Most people start with "I don't know what I need" — that's exactly what the call is for.

Is this actually free?

Yes. I'm paid by the insurer if you decide to move forward, never by you directly.

What if I already have coverage?

I'll review what you have and tell you honestly if it's already suitable — I won't manufacture a reason to switch.

Do you only sell one company's products?

No. I'm independent and compare 25+ Canadian insurers to find what actually fits.

What should I bring to the call?

Nothing required — just be ready to talk about your family, income and any coverage you already have.

Can we talk in Punjabi or Hindi?

Yes — whatever's most comfortable for you.
